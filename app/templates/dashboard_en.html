Partners

Partners are other people in the notification group. Each partner may have their own contact methods and trusted persons.

Who receives a message?

The person assigned to the submitting trusted person is not notified. All other active group members with a confirmed contact method are considered. The trusted person learns neither names nor the number of recipients.
